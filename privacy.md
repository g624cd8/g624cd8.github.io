# Liz privacy policy

Liz is used by one person only, on their own computer.
With their permission it reads, drafts and sends their Gmail and finds and reads their Google Drive files, only when they ask.
Their data is processed on their computer and by the services that power the assistant (Google, Anthropic, Deepgram, Cartesia). It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything else.
Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
